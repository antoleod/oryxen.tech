03 / Engineering principles

AI without control is another operational risk.

01

Connect to real information

Use authorized systems and sources. Clearly indicate when evidence is missing.

02

Permission before action

Separate recommendations from execution; require approval for sensitive changes.

03

Keep work observable

Record what ran, what changed and where errors occurred, with appropriate access controls.

04

Deliver something maintainable

Readable code, scoped integrations, documentation and a clean handoff.

04 / Let's build something useful

Tell us which part of your workflow should be easier.

Describe the current process, the bottleneck and what a successful outcome would look like. We can help identify whether automation, AI or simpler software is the right fit.
